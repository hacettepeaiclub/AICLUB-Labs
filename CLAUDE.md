# Claude Code instructions

## Turkish copy

Before writing or editing any Turkish text (`src/i18n/tr.ts`,
`src/i18n/labs/tr/`, the `tr` entries of `src/components/lab/journeyCopy.ts`,
or Turkish strings inside components), read:

- `docs/language/tr-style.md`: register ("sen"), how instructions are
  written, what not to say, number formats.
- `docs/language/tr-terminology.md`: the approved term for each concept, its
  definition, the contexts where an alternative is correct, and its sources.

Identify the concept from the control, the chart and the calculation before
choosing a word, and do not replace terms by bulk search-and-replace. A term
that is not in the glossary is proposed to the project owner with its sources
before it is used. Do not change an experiment's mathematics or behaviour
while editing its copy.
